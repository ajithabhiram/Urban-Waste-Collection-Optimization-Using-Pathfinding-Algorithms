Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Garbage Collection Route Planner?

It's a web-based tool that optimizes garbage collection routes using advanced algorithms, helping reduce costs and environmental impact.

Which algorithms are used?

The planner uses Nearest Neighbor, Dijkstra, Bellman-Ford, Johnson's, Floyd-Warshall, and Ant Colony Optimization algorithms.

How does it handle truck capacity?

The system tracks garbage collected and routes the truck to the disposal point when capacity is exceeded, ensuring efficient operations.

Is it free to use?

We offer a free tier with basic features. Sign up to explore premium features for larger-scale operations.

Can I integrate it with my existing systems?

Yes, our API (available soon) will allow integration with your waste management systems. Contact us for details.
